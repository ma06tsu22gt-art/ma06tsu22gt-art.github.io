FAQ

Is it free?

The free version lets you track up to 3 items. The unlimited plan (monthly or one-time purchase) removes the item limit; features are otherwise the same. The free version shows banner ads.

Where is my data stored?

Your records stay on your device and are never sent to a server. You can export a backup file, for example when you change phones.
